Family Legacy

A goldsmith tradition carried into a modern private acquisitions office.

The Montford family first earned its reputation in the old European gold trade, where skilled hands, accurate weights, and a careful eye meant everything.

In Antwerp, the family worked as goldsmiths and developed a standard of trade built on measured judgment rather than spectacle. When the family came to the United States in the late 1800s, they brought more than a profession with them. They brought a way of doing business: careful evaluation, respect for the owner, and a belief that value should be handled with discipline.

Over time, the work evolved from making and repairing precious-metal pieces to evaluating, purchasing, and advising on inherited collections. Montford Reserve Metals continues that tradition with the same core principles: know what you are looking at, communicate clearly, document the process, and pay fairly.
