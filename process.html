Private acquisitions protocol

A clear process for selling estate jewelry, bullion, and inherited metals.

Montford Reserve Metals begins with a private review request, confirms the right intake path, documents inspection carefully, and communicates offers and settlement in a measured way from start to finish.

Request a private review Open client portal

01 | Private review request

Start with a summary, not a shipment.

Begin with a concise description of the collection, any known purity or provenance, approximate weight, and preferred contact details. This gives the office enough context to determine whether the submission is a fit before valuables move anywhere.

  • Provide a clear item summary and any useful family or estate background.
  • Treat the public estimate as an indicative starting point only.
  • Wait for office confirmation before arranging shipment or meeting.

02 | Intake coordination

Confirm the correct handoff before valuables move.

Once a request is reviewed, the office confirms the right intake path for the file. That may mean shipment, scheduled delivery, or a request for better documentation before the items are moved.

  • Do not mail items without prior communication and a reference on file.
  • Package material securely and keep a private record of what was sent.
  • Include identifying information only as requested for the file.

03 | Inspection and pricing

Final offers follow actual inspection.

On receipt, items are logged, weighed, tested when appropriate, and reviewed in light of recoverable metal value, condition, and the office's current buy pricing. Claimed purity alone does not set the final figure.

  • Status milestones may appear in the client portal once a file is opened.
  • Final offers are issued only after inspection is complete.
  • If a submission is declined, the office records that disposition clearly.

04 | Offer, payment, and closeout

Accepted offers are settled clearly and recorded to the file.

If an offer is accepted, payment is issued by the approved method and the settlement record is tied to the file. If an offer is declined, return handling and timing are communicated in writing.

  • Accepted amount, payment method, and settlement status are recorded for the file.
  • Identity and ownership verification may be required before payment.
  • Each file is closed with a written status trail rather than informal text-only updates.

Office standards

What clients should expect

  • Measured communication instead of pressure or hard-sell language.
  • Manual review before estate metals or bullion move further.
  • A written status trail from intake through payment or return.
  • Clear separation between indicative estimate and final offer.

Expected cadence

How a well-run file should move

  • Acknowledge new intake requests within one business day.
  • Log received parcels promptly once physically in hand.
  • Issue final offers after inspection and internal review.
  • Record payment or return status promptly once action occurs.

Director oversight
