# Brand Foundation

Status: approved foundation

## Primary Recommendation

- Business name: `Montford Reserve Metals`
- Descriptor: `Buyers of Gold, Silver & Fine Jewelry`
- Public title: `Director of Private Acquisitions`
- Public face: `James Montford`

## Brand Position

Montford Reserve Metals should read as a discreet, family-led precious-metals buying house that handles estate jewelry, bullion, inherited collections, and fine metal assets with composure and documented care. The brand should feel more like a private office than a storefront.

## Why The Name Works

- `Montford` sounds inherited, affluent, and generational.
- `Reserve` gives the business institutional weight and financial gravity.
- `Metals` adds immediate category clarity without dragging the brand down into pawn-shop language.
- The full name feels established and premium without sounding trendy or overly invented.

## Brand Promises

- Discreet communication
- Calm, documented process
- Fair and professional valuation
- Respect for inherited and sentimental pieces
- No rushed or high-pressure tone

## Customer Impression Goal

The customer should feel that Montford Reserve Metals is the kind of firm that has seen major estates, family inheritances, bullion holdings, and complicated valuation situations before. The business should signal patience, solvency, and composure.

## What The Brand Is Not

- Not a pawn shop
- Not a startup that happens to buy gold
- Not a flashy luxury brand
- Not a loud “cash for gold” funnel
- Not a cold, robotic financial institution

## Brand Descriptor Options

- `Buyers of Gold, Silver & Fine Jewelry`
- `Private Precious Metals Office`
- `Estate Jewelry, Bullion & Inherited Collection Buyers`

## Approval Status

- `Montford Reserve Metals` is the approved public-facing company name.
- The brand should appear everywhere in full.
- `Buyers of Gold, Silver & Fine Jewelry` is the approved descriptor.
- `James Montford` is the approved public face and `Director of Private Acquisitions`.
