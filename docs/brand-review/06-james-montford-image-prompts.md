## Character Goal

James Montford should feel like the Matthew McConaughey of finance:

- relaxed confidence
- warm charisma
- expensive but never flashy
- established, solvent, and hard to rattle
- original character energy, not a celebrity clone

## Identity Lock

Every image of James Montford should preserve these core traits:

- male, mid-to-late 40s
- slightly wavy medium-brown to dark-blond hair with only light gray at the temples
- healthy sun-touched complexion
- leaner, more youthful face shape with strong jaw and calm, intelligent eyes
- handsome but seasoned, not silver-fox and not over-modeled
- warm, private, old-money confidence
- tailored navy or charcoal wardrobe
- clean, understated accessories only
- a relaxed half-smile with a hint of mischief rather than a stern executive expression

## Global Constraints

- no celebrity likeness
- no text
- no watermark
- no crypto-bro or influencer styling
- no flashy watches, chains, or loud luxury cues
- no pawn-shop energy
- no exaggerated grin
- no gimmicks, cash piles, or casino vibes
- no heavy gray hair or dominant silver-fox styling
- do not let `mansuit.webp` dominate the face or age

## Iteration Notes

If the first generation misses the target, adjust one thing at a time:

- too old: reduce gray hair and facial age lines slightly
- too much mansuit influence: darken the hair, reduce gray, slim the face slightly, and increase warmth
- too young: add more season and restraint, less youthful shine
- too banker-stiff: open the collar, soften expression, increase warmth
- too fashion-model: reduce polish and increase realism
- too salesy: remove smile intensity and reduce forward posture
