# Family Legacy Story

Status: medium version approved

## Short Version

The Montford family traces its craft to goldsmiths working in Antwerp in the early 1700s. Known for precision, patience, and honest dealing, the family later immigrated to the United States in the late 1800s and carried that bench-trained discipline into the trade of heirloom jewelry, estate metal, and private valuation. Today, James Montford continues that legacy through a private buying business built on fairness, discretion, and documented process.

## Medium Version

Approved working story version.

The Montford family first earned its reputation in the old European gold trade, where skilled hands, accurate weights, and a careful eye meant everything. In Antwerp, the family worked as goldsmiths and developed a standard of trade built on measured judgment rather than spectacle.

When the family came to the United States in the late 1800s, they brought more than a profession with them. They brought a way of doing business: careful evaluation, respect for the owner, and a belief that value should be handled with discipline. Over time, the work evolved from making and repairing precious-metal pieces to evaluating, purchasing, and advising on inherited collections.

Montford Reserve Metals is presented as the modern continuation of that tradition. The tools have changed, but the principles remain the same: know what you are looking at, communicate clearly, document the process, and pay fairly.
