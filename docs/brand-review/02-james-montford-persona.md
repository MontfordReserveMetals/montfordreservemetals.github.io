# James Montford Persona

Status: approved persona direction, portrait anchor approved

## Role

`James Montford` is the public face of Montford Reserve Metals. He is the man who embodies the brand's trust signal: wealthy, composed, experienced, and easy to believe.

## Archetype

The target is the Matthew McConaughey of finance:

- relaxed confidence
- natural charm
- expensive without looking flashy
- seasoned rather than stiff
- warm enough to trust, selective enough to respect

This should feel like an original character archetype, not a celebrity lookalike. The energy should come from Felix plus McConaughey-style ease, not from a generic silver-fox finance model.

## Core Persona Traits

- Age: mid-to-late 40s
- Demeanor: unhurried, controlled, observant
- Presence: attractive, well-traveled, calm under pressure
- Social signal: old money, not new money
- Style signal: tailored and expensive, but never loud

## Visual Direction

- Slightly wavy medium-brown to dark-blond hair with light gray only at the temples
- Healthy, sun-touched complexion
- Lean handsome face, strong jaw, and expressive eyes
- Relaxed half-smile with a hint of playful confidence, never a broad grin
- Tailored navy or charcoal suit
- Crisp white shirt, usually open collar
- Understated gold watch or signet ring
- Paneled office, leather, wood, ledgers, and framed documents

## Performance Notes

He should appear as though he has been around real money, inherited collections, and valuable metal assets for years. He is not trying to impress people. That is why he is impressive.

## Voice Notes

- Speaks clearly and slowly
- Uses calm, direct language
- Does not oversell
- Treats precious metals as both financial assets and family property
- Makes clients feel looked after, not worked over

## Do Not Drift Into

- Celebrity clone territory
- Hard-selling salesman posture
- Crypto-bro or finance-influencer energy
- Flashy watches, chains, or status props
- A severe private-banker look with no warmth
- A dominant silver-fox look like `mansuit.webp`

## Sample Introduction

James Montford is the current steward of a family tradition in precious metals, bringing old-world judgment and modern discretion to the purchase of estate jewelry, bullion, and inherited gold and silver.
