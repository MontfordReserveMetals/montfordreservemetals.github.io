Terms of review

These terms explain how preliminary estimates, inspection, offers, and settlement are handled.

Montford Reserve Metals handles estate jewelry, bullion, inherited metals, and related property through a documented review process. These terms set expectations for indicative estimates, inspection, final offers, ownership verification, payment, and return handling.

Important notice

Additional identification, ownership verification, reporting, or hold requirements may apply depending on item type, transaction size, and jurisdiction.

Estimates

Public estimates are indicative only.

Any number shown through the website estimator or discussed before physical review is a non-binding preliminary indication. Final pricing depends on actual inspection, tested purity, recoverable weight, market conditions, and Montford Reserve Metals pricing at the time of review.

Inspection rights

The office may reclassify items after receipt and testing.

Submitted materials may be weighed, tested, sorted, or otherwise examined to determine whether they match the submitted description. If an item differs materially from the claimed karat, weight, or type, the final offer may be adjusted or declined.

Eligibility

Clients must have lawful authority to sell the submitted items.

By submitting a request or sending property for review, the client represents that they are the lawful owner of the items or are otherwise authorized to sell them. The office may request identification, proof of ownership, or additional verification before proceeding.

Offer acceptance

Final offers carry a stated validity period.

Once a final offer is issued, it remains open only for the period stated by the office. If that window expires without acceptance, the office may revise, withdraw, or decline the offer.

Settlement

Payment method, timing, and any hold period are disclosed clearly.

Approved payments may be sent by the methods the office supports at the time of settlement. Processing timelines may vary based on banking cutoffs, verification requirements, fraud review, or legal compliance.

Returns and declines

Declined or unaccepted items require a written return procedure.

If the office declines a submission or the client rejects a final offer, return handling follows the stated process for that file. Timing, any applicable fees, and the duration of any holding period are communicated in writing.

Key principles

  • Preliminary estimate disclaimer
  • Inspection and adjustment rights
  • Ownership and identification requirements
  • Offer expiration terms
  • Payment timing and return handling

Montford Reserve Metals does not promise

  • Guaranteed prices before inspection
  • Instant payment before required verification is complete
  • Acceptance of every submission presented for review
  • Terms that override applicable legal or reporting obligations
  • Return timing that has not been confirmed for the file
