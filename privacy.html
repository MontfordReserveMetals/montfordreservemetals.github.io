Privacy policy

Private transactions require private handling.

This policy explains how Montford Reserve Metals handles website inquiries, shipment-related records, client portal access, and communications connected to valuation, inspection, offers, and settlement.

Scope of this policy

This policy applies to information submitted through the website, by email or telephone, through the client portal, and through records created during review, inspection, payment, or return handling.

What is collected

Inquiry, contact, shipment, and portal data may all become part of the file.

The office may collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, mailing details, item descriptions, estimated weights and purity claims, shipment references, portal login information, and records of communications related to an inquiry, active review, or completed transaction.

How it is used

Information is used to review requests, communicate with clients, and maintain records.

Data may be used to respond to inquiries, evaluate submissions, provide status updates, authenticate client portal access, document inspection and settlement activity, and satisfy internal recordkeeping or legal compliance requirements.

Service providers

Third-party services may support the site, portal, communications, and payment workflow.

The office may use third-party service providers to host the website, deliver secure portal access, manage communications, support shipments, or assist with payment processing. Information is shared only to the extent reasonably necessary for those services or as required by law.

Retention and security

Files are retained only as long as operationally or legally necessary.

The office retains records for as long as needed to manage active submissions, resolve disputes, complete transactions, and satisfy legal obligations. Reasonable administrative and technical safeguards are used, but no online system can be guaranteed absolutely secure.

Client rights

Clients may request corrections or ask questions about how their information is handled.

If a client wants to update contact details, correct an error, or ask how information is being handled, they may contact the private office using the information shown below.

Website changes

This policy may change as the business or its service providers evolve.

If Montford Reserve Metals changes how client information is collected, stored, or shared, this policy may be revised so the public description remains accurate.

Information may come from

  • Website inquiry and estimate-request forms
  • Email, telephone, and mailed correspondence
  • Shipment and delivery records
  • Client portal access and status history

Information may be shared with

  • Website, portal, and communication providers
  • Shipment, payment, or banking partners as needed
  • Identity or compliance providers when verification is required
  • Authorities when disclosure is required by law
